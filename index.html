Four words to know

Authentication
Checking who you are. A second factor, such as an approval on a registered phone, adds a check.
Authorisation
Checking what you’re allowed to open or do after your identity is established.
Endpoint
A device people use, such as a laptop. A managed device is maintained by the organisation.
Network zone
A group of systems with similar access needs. Policy controls which connections can cross between zones.
